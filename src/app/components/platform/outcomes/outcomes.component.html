The quality challenge is bigger than test execution.

Enterprise applications rarely exist as a single system. A customer journey may move through a web application, mobile experience, APIs, databases, integration services and legacy platforms. A change that appears small at the UI layer can affect a process, calculation or business transaction.

TesQuirel is designed around this reality. The platform connects the activities teams need to understand, design, execute and interpret quality across the application landscape — not just automate individual test steps.
