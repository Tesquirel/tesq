From business intent to release confidence

TesQuirel starts where quality truly begins—with business intent, customer outcomes, risks and acceptance criteria, not test scripts.  Business requirements are transformed into meaningful test scenarios, test data and risk-based coverage across the complete application landscape. From Web, API and Mobile to data, integrations and legacy systems, testing is executed as a connected quality process rather than isolated activities.  Every test, defect, result and piece of evidence remains traceable to the underlying business requirement and release risk. AI assists teams in designing and optimising tests, while human governance remains accountable for coverage, risk and release decisions. The result is more than test execution—it is evidence-based release confidence, enabling organisations to release faster with greater coverage, controlled risk and measurable quality.
