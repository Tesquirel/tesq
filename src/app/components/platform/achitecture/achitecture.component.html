Architecture principle

Enterprise quality problems are rarely isolated to one testing layer. Requirements may live in one system, test assets in another, automation in another and execution evidence somewhere else. The result is fragmented visibility.

TesQuirel's architecture is designed around connected quality information: what the business expects, what needs to be tested, how it is executed, what changed, what failed and what evidence supports the release decision.
