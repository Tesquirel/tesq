The Insurance QA Challenge

Insurance platforms are inherently complex, governed by regulations, and highly configurable. QA teams face persistent challenges such as:

  • Frequent product, pricing, and rule changes
  • Complex policy lifecycle workflows
  • High dependency on legacy core systems
  • Multi-channel customer journeys (agent, web, mobile)
  • Stringent regulatory and audit requirements
  • Long regression cycles impacting time-to-market

Traditional script-based automation struggles to keep pace with this complexity.
