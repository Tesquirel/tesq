Why TrustVerse Exists

AI has moved from creating content to influencing decisions, executing tasks and working as an agent. This changes the quality problem. AI systems can be based on probabilities to context, able to change and capable of doing actions across many systems. TrustVerse sees Trust as something that is built than just said. Accuracy is still important. But, it is just one part. A trusted AI system also needs limits in decisions it can take independently. Major actions or decisions to be verified by someone who is responsible and accountable.

The TrustVerse Principle

AI quality is not completed until its decisions and actions can be understood, controlled, proven and owned. TrustVerse brings these parts together into a plan for AI-based Quality Engineering and checking. It is for AI applications, RAG systems, LLM-driven workflows and agent systems where companies need to feel sure during and after putting them to use.
