Health-Tech Platform QA Challenges

Health-Tech platforms operate under unique quality constraints:

  • Rapid feature evolution with frequent releases
  • Complex user journeys across patients, providers, and administrators
  • Regulatory and compliance requirements (HIPAA, data privacy etc.,)
  • High dependency on integrations and data accuracy
  • Zero tolerance for defects affecting patient experience or data integrity

Traditional scripted automation struggles to keep pace with the speed, scale, and regulatory sensitivity of modern Health-Tech platforms.

TesQuirel’s Platform-First QA Strategy

TesQuirel brings a platform-centric quality approach, combining no-script automation and AI-driven test intelligence to ensure Health-Tech platforms scale reliably without compromising safety or compliance.
