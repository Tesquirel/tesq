From Activity Metrics to Confidence Signals

Traditional reporting emphasizes numbers: test cases executed, passed or failed, defects opened and defects closed. Those measures remain useful, but they do not by themselves answer the executive question: "How confident are we in this release?"

TesQuirel's platform model adds context. What business intent was covered? Which journeys were exercised? Which application layers were included? What changed between runs? Which failures remain unexplained? What evidence supports the decision?

This creates a more meaningful conversation between QA, engineering, product and business stakeholders.

A Practical Outcome for Enterprise Teams.

For an enterprise with modern applications alongside legacy systems, the objective is not to replace every existing tool overnight. It is to create a more connected quality model and progressively automate work that is repeatable, high-volume or difficult to validate manually. TesQuirel can therefore be introduced through a focused business journey, application or regression problem and expanded as the organization gains confidence.
